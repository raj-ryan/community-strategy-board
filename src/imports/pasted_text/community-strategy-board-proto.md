Copy-paste prompt for Figma Make
Create a responsive desktop prototype for a Community Strategy Board that helps first-year international graduate students navigate unfamiliar course systems and academic expectations.
The board should feel like a calm, student-built FigJam board rather than a corporate dashboard. Use a warm off-white background, subtle dotted grid, thin gray borders, minimal shadows, rounded cards, and restrained pastel accents. Use Noto Sans throughout.

Strategy 1
Card front
Check Week 0 and Week 1 tasks early
Some assignments are due before the first class.
First week Deadlines
Contributed by Emma · HCDE, Year 2
Useful across programs

Expanded view
Why this helps
Students may assume coursework begins after the first class. Instructors may assign introductions, readings, surveys, or software setup before the first meeting.
Try this
Open Canvas as soon as the course appears.
Check Modules, Assignments, Announcements, and the syllabus.
Look for anything labeled Week 0, Before class, Getting started, or Required setup.
Best time to use it
Before the quarter begins and again two or three days before the first class.

Why this helps
Students may not know that Canvas deadlines can appear automatically in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.
Try this
Open the Calendar page in Canvas.
Copy the Canvas calendar-feed link.
Subscribe to the feed in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.
Best time to use it
At the beginning of every quarter, after all courses have appeared in Canvas.

Strategy 3
Card front
Use office hours before there is a problem
Clarify expectations, discuss feedback, or test an idea early.
Communication Instructor support

Why this helps
In some educational cultures, contacting a professor may feel formal or appropriate only when something is wrong. At UW, office hours can also be used for clarification, feedback, or early discussion.
Try this
Bring one specific question, draft, or idea.
Briefly explain what you have already checked or attempted.
Ask what a stronger next step would look like.
Best time to use it
Before a major assignment, after receiving feedback, or when expectations remain unclear.
Community evidence

68 reported still using office hours or similar instructor support.

Strategy 4
Card front
Protect time for important work
Not everything important has a deadline or grade.
Planning Weekly habit
Contributed anonymously · M.Arch, Year 2
Most useful for studio and project-based courses
♡ 35 140 tried 95 still using
View strategy →
Expanded view
Why this helps
Readings, software setup, feedback review, office hours, project planning, and relationship-building may not appear in the Canvas To-Do list, making them easy to postpone.
Try this
Choose one recurring planning time each week.
Add one or two important but non-urgent tasks to your calendar.
Give each task a specific time rather than leaving it on a general list.
Examples
Review feedback before the next assignment.
Install required software before it is needed.
Prepare questions for office hours.
Plan the next stage of a long-term project.
Community evidence
140 students tried this strategy.
95 reported still using it after several weeks.

Suggested prototype interactions
Create these clickable flows:
Select I keep missing deadlines → show Strategies 1, 2, and 4.
Select I don’t know how professors communicate → show Strategies 1 and 3.
Select Architecture → prioritize Strategy 4.
Select Public Health → prioritize Strategy 3.
Click View strategy → open the expanded side panel.
Click Add to My Quarter → add the card to the saved-strategy list.
Click Thank contributor → change the action to Thanked.
Click Share a strategy → open the moderated contribution form.
The overall design should feel calm, practical, peer-supported, and easy to scan. The strategy itself should always be more visually prominent than likes, metrics, contributor information, or social interactions.
